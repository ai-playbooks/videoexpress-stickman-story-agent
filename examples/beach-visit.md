## Generate the master character first

Before any beach scene, generate a dedicated character image using this prompt:

> Polished stylized 3D animated-feature character reference, one full-body stickman named Milo, centered in a relaxed three-quarter standing pose on a plain light-gray studio background. White spherical head with two expressive teal eyes, dark curved eyebrows and a friendly small smile; compact torso, rounded articulated black limbs, exactly two separate arms with two clearly visible empty hands resting away from the torso, exactly two legs with both feet fully visible. Turquoise short-sleeved shirt with subtle cotton texture, coral-red shorts, yellow sandals. Stable grounded stance, readable silhouette, consistent rounded proportions, soft even studio illumination, gentle contact shadows beneath the sandals, smooth matte head and limbs, clear material depth, generous headroom and space around the entire body. The only subject is Milo; his beach bag and beach setting belong in the later scene images.

Inspect anatomy and costume, then save the selected image as 'A Little Day by the Sea — Master Character'. Enable Consistent Character and select this exact saved master as Reference Photo 1 for all eight scenes, starting with scene 1. Verify it before each scene submission. Keep beach scene images separate from the master; never substitute them as the character reference. Introduce the yellow bag as a scene prop in the positions described below.

## Narration

1. After a busy morning, Milo reached the beach, ready for a peaceful day beside the sparkling sea.
2. Beneath a bright striped umbrella, he set down his bag and finally took a slow, peaceful breath.
3. When the first cool wave curled around his feet, Milo felt his busy morning drift far away.
4. A beautiful spiral shell waited in the wet sand, turning an ordinary wave into a small discovery.
5. Nearby, a little builder needed help with a sandy tower, and Milo was happy to lend a hand.
6. With a little patience and a few careful pats, their sandy castle finally stood tall and proud.
7. As the afternoon turned golden, three new friends sat together, watching the sea shimmer beyond their castle.
8. Milo headed home with sandy sandals, a warm goodbye, and a heart that felt lighter than before.
