# Example request

```text
I want you to create a narrated VideoExpress story about a struggling student who discovers that small daily habits can transform his future. Use my currently signed-in VideoExpress account and its included generation features. Make it 9:16, 2D stickman style, a concise complete story, and use the default Beau Whitaker narrator. You may generate and correct the required images, integrated narration, and videos; assemble, review, Auto Align, and save the project. Keep public-gallery sharing off. Do not purchase or upgrade anything, accept new terms, delete existing assets, change account-wide settings, switch providers, or publish publicly. Export the finished video after saving.
```

The standard intake asks for story/topic, aspect ratio (Vertical 9:16 or Landscape 16:9 only), and narrator. It does not offer 1:1 or other ratios. Beau Whitaker is the narrator default, so production can continue with that voice when the user does not choose another.

The agent should open or focus a visible supported browser first, honoring the user's selected session and recovering through available browser alternatives when necessary. After any explicitly requested account confirmation, create a connected story of short narration lines and continue routine production without per-clip approval requests. Choose enough short lines to complete the story without a fixed runtime. For every reviewed source image, create Beau Whitaker system-voice narration inside VideoExpress's integrated narration dialog, then generate the matching narrated 2D stickman clip. Pipeline scene submissions, defer detailed QA until the final pass, assemble using right-click Add to Timeline, run Auto Align, measure actual runtime, save and verify the final export.

Example opening beats (durations must be measured after generation):

| Scene | One-line narration | Matching video action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Every morning, Milo stared at his unfinished homework. | Milo looks down at an open homework notebook. |
| 02 | Today, he decided to finish just one small task. | Milo picks up his pencil and starts one problem. |
| 03 | One tiny checkmark gave him a reason to smile. | Milo marks the completed problem and smiles. |

Each row produces one integrated narrated video clip. Continue with connected beats through the story’s ending; these three rows are only an opening example.
